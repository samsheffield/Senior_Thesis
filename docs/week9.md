## T O D A Y

### Senior Thesis Midterm Presentations (10/21)

Students developing a Senior Thesis project are responsible for creating a brief slideshow presentation (maximum 10 minutes) that explains what the project is, the ideas behind it, and your goals as designers and artists. You will present this in class after Fall Break.

In addition to looking ahead, your presentation should also provide context by citing relevant research and prototyping from this semester to show an understanding of where the work originates.
