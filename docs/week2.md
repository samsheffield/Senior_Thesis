# Fall '25 Senior Thesis!

## T O D A Y

### Self introductions
- **Who are you?**  
    - Name, pronouns
    - Major/minor
    - Where are you from?

- **Tell us about 1-2 interests or hobbies**
    - What are you interested in?

- **Show 2–3 examples of your own work** that you are excited about.  
  - Tell us what you like, or find successful about it.  
  - This can include **collaborative work**.  
- **Show 2–3 examples of work by others** that you find inspiring.  
  - Tell us what you find successful or inspiring about it.  


### Interested in collaborating/experimenting with others?
Today's a good day to start having conversations. Remember, in the first half of the semester, you are not committed to any particular thesis idea. You can try collaborating on something small with others in this class to just get things started.

# Homework

## Studio Hours 

**Goal**: Get used to our weekly peer check-in/review structure.

**Time**: Plan to spend about 4–5 hours.

**Deliverable**: No submission for this one.

### Assignment Description:
Use this time to begin (or continue) working on something connected to what you might want to do for your thesis. You’ll be sharing your progress in a small group, so just make sure you have something to talk about or show.

There aren’t any strict requirements for what you work on right now, just make sure it’s _not_ homework for another class.
