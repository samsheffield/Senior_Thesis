# Fall '24 Senior Thesis!

## Senior Thesis Project Milestone 2 Reviews
We'll share out a summary of your work in the second half of the semester as a group. Please show highlights of what you've been working on and discuss your current plans for continuing individual or collaborative work in the Spring semester.

Be prepared for 15-20 minutes (including feedback and questions).


## Project Proposal Presentations (Due Next Week, 12/10)
Students will be responsible for preparing a presentation demonstrating the work you are proposing as your Senior Thesis Project. 

This presentation can be organized using any slideshow tool such as Powerpoint, Google Slides, or Keynote. It will include examples of relevant research, inspiration, prototypes and experiments, key or concept art, aand a proposed timeline for continued development in the Spring semester. 

- Presentations can be completed collaboratively if you are working on a team, with each member providing insight into their particular role (or roles) in the project’s development.

- __If you intend to work on multiple projects (a mix of independent work and colaborative work), you will be required to prepare a presentation for each project.__

Detailed presentation guidelines [here](presentations.md).

### Milestone 2 Documentation
Next week, you will also submit documentation of the culmination of your progress and work completed during the second half of the semester. 

The documentation will consist of a folder including work samples demonstrating the emerging body or work, prototypes, or other exploratory experiments that you have been working on during the second half of the semester.

Please make sure things are shared in _friendly_ file formats (for example: jpegs, gifs, pdfs, mp4s). Unity project files are acceptable, but PC builds are preferred (if possible). Otherwise, create a small exported video clearly demonstrating what you been working on. 

Organize these files into folders as you see fit.
