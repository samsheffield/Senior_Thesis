## Homework

### Self Introductions (Due next week)  

#### What to prepare  
- **Who are you?**  
    - Name, pronouns
    - Major/minor
    - Where are you from?

- **Tell us about 1-2 interests or hobbies**
    - What are you interested in?

- **Show 2–3 examples of your own work** that you are excited about.  
  - Tell us what you like, or find successful about it.  
  - This can include **collaborative work**.  
- **Show 2–3 examples of work by others** that you find inspiring.  
  - Tell us what you find successful or inspiring about it.  

#### Time  
- Be prepared for **no more than 10 minutes**.  
- Please manage your time carefully when considering what to show.

#### Format  
- You do **not** need to create a formal slideshow presentation, unless you find it helpful for organization.  

#### Important  
- **Do not create a presentation pitching ideas or plans for thesis.** 
- You will have a dedicated opportunity to present thesis ideas later in the semester.  

#### Deliverable
- You do not need to submit anything for a grade on this assignment
